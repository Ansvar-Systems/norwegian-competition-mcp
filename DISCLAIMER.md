## Currency and Staleness

- Database updates are **manual** and may lag official publications by weeks or months
- Norwegian competition enforcement changes through new decisions, legislative amendments, and court rulings
- A decision marked "final" may have been appealed or overturned since the last database update
- Fine amounts and outcomes may change on appeal
- This Tool does NOT automatically track appeals or amendments

## Coverage Gaps

This Tool does **NOT** include:

1. **Full legal text** -- contains representative summaries only
2. **Konkurranseklagenemnda decisions** -- Competition Appeals Board rulings are not covered
3. **Court judgments** -- District court and Supreme Court competition rulings are not included
4. **EU/EEA competition decisions** -- EFTA Surveillance Authority and European Commission decisions are covered separately by the EU Regulations MCP
5. **Leniency applications** -- Confidential leniency program details are not included
6. **Ongoing investigations** -- Only published final decisions are covered
7. **Historical decisions before 2004** -- Limited coverage of decisions under the previous competition act (konkurranseloven av 1993)
